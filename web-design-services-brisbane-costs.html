Timelines and What Affects Delays

Timing follows the same logic as scope. A simple brochure site is commonly around 4 to 10 weeks. A WordPress or small business site usually takes around 6 to 10 weeks. An eCommerce or custom build often starts from about 12 weeks up to several months. The published page notes that timelines depend most on how quickly content and feedback come back. If approvals are slow or content is still unclear, the date moves. Ask which pages and functions are included at launch. Check what content must be supplied before design starts.
